Public Companies Rethink How They Reach Retail Investors

By Business Desk Staff · Updated 2 hours ago · 4 min read

As self-directed trading platforms have changed how individuals research and buy stocks, investor relations teams at smaller listed companies are revisiting an old question: how does the story reach the people who might actually own the shares?

Advisors say the traditional route of broker calls, conferences and roadshows is hard to measure and slow to scale, and that more IR budgets are moving toward channels where the results of each dollar can be tracked.
